## About

Mavrik has three main workspaces: **Chat**, **Monitor**, and **Recon**.

### **Chat**

**Mavrik Chat is the natural language workspace.**

Investigate digital asset markets by asking about stablecoin flows, DEX activity, lending, token transfers, wallet-level behavior, and more. Start from an example prompt or write your own.

Free users receive 10 prompts per month, Pro users receive 50 prompts per month, and Enterprise plans include a custom prompt amount based on your team’s needs.

### **Monitor**

**Mavrik Monitor is the always-on tracking workspace.**

Save useful Chat visualizations and keep them up-to-date with onchain activity. Use Monitor to follow important trends over time without rerunning the same analysis.

Free users can browse Featured Monitors. Pro and Enterprise users can create Custom Monitors.

### **Recon**

**Mavrik Recon is the client intelligence workspace.**

Understand customer wallet behavior across on-platform and off-platform activity. Use Recon to identify external balances, transaction patterns, protocol usage, and opportunities for timely action.

Recon is available on Enterprise plans only.  [Contact us](https://www.validationcloud.io/contact)
