---
title: "platform.getTotalStake"
---

Get the total amount of tokens staked on the requested Subnet.

This method will use `90` [Compute Units](/nodeapi/about/billing).

***

## Reference: [Here](https://docs.avax.network/reference/avalanchego/p-chain/api#platformgettotalstake)

## **Parameters**

`None`&#x20;

## Returns

* `stake` - int - The the total amount of nAVAX staked on the Primary Network.

```json
// Result
{
  "jsonrpc": "2.0",
  "result": {
    "stake": "279825917679866811",
    "weight": "279825917679866811"
  },
  "id": 1
}
```
