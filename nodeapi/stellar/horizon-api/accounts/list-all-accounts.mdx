---
title: "List all Accounts"
openapi: "/nodeapi/openapi/horizon-openapi-vc.json GET /accounts"
---

This endpoint lists accounts by one of four filters : signer, asset, liquidity pool or sponsor.

This method will use `20` [Compute Units](/nodeapi/about/billing).
