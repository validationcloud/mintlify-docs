## Key Features

### **Click-to-Deploy Non-Custodial Staking**

We have designed our product to enable an on-demand staking experience for the deployment of up to 100 Ethereum validators per transaction.

### **Distributed Validator Technology (DVT) Support**

Distributed Validators (DVs) on Ethereum improve decentralization and security by spreading validation responsibilities across multiple independent nodes and can also work across multiple operators. This reduces the risk of single points of failure, slashing penalties, and collusion, while increasing fault tolerance.

Validation Cloud's Ethereum staking user interface and API offers out of the box support for creating Distributed Ethereum Validators (DVs) via [SSV Network](https://docs.ssv.network/learn/introduction/).

We also have extensive experience deploying and maintaining [Obol](https://obol.org/) clusters and associated Distributed Validators.

### **Enterprise-Grade Security**

Our staking platform is audited and penetration tested by third parties from the commission smart contract to the staking API and user interface. The entire platform adheres to the stringent SOC 2 compliance standards set forth by the AICPA.

Our dedicated team of experts is available around the clock to assist you with any technical concerns or queries, ensuring a seamless experience with our staking product.
