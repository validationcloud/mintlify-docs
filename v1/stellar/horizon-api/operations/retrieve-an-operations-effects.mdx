---
title: "Retrieve an Operation's Effects"
openapi: "/v1/openapi/horizon-openapi-vc.json GET /operations/{id}/effects"
---

This endpoint returns the effects of a specific operation.

This method will use `20` [Compute Units](/v1/about/billing).
