---
title: "book_offers"
---

Retrieves a list of offers between two currencies, also known as an order book. The response omits unfunded offers and reports how much of each remaining offer is funded.

This method will use `40` [Compute Units](/v1/about/billing).

***

## Reference: [Here](https://xrpl.org/docs/references/http-websocket-apis/public-api-methods/path-and-order-book-methods/book_offers)

## **Parameters**

* `taker_gets` (object, required) The asset the account taking the offer would receive, as a currency object without an amount (`currency`, plus `issuer` for tokens).
* `taker_pays` (object, required) The asset the account taking the offer would pay, in the same format.
* `domain` (string, optional) The ledger entry ID of a permissioned domain. If provided, return offers from the corresponding permissioned DEX instead of the open DEX.
* `ledger_hash` (string, optional) The unique hash of the ledger version to use.
* `ledger_index` (number or string, optional) The ledger index of the ledger to use, or a shortcut string such as `validated`, `closed` or `current`.
* `limit` (number, optional) The maximum number of offers to return. The response may include fewer results.
* `taker` (string, optional) The address of an account to use as a perspective. The response includes this account's offers even if they are unfunded.

## Returns

* `ledger_current_index` (number) Omitted if `ledger_index` is provided.
* `ledger_index` (number) The ledger index of the ledger version used.
* `ledger_hash` (string) The identifying hash of the ledger version used.
* `offers` (array) Array of Offer ledger entries, each with the additional fields `owner_funds` (how much of `TakerGets` the owner can trade), `quality` (`taker_pays` divided by `taker_gets`) and, for partially funded offers, `taker_gets_funded` and `taker_pays_funded`.
