---
title: "deposit_authorized"
---

Indicates whether one account is authorized to send payments directly to another, taking Deposit Authorization and any preauthorizations into account.

This method will use `20` [Compute Units](/v1/about/billing).

***

## Reference: [Here](https://xrpl.org/docs/references/http-websocket-apis/public-api-methods/path-and-order-book-methods/deposit_authorized)

## **Parameters**

* `source_account` (string, required) The sender of a possible payment.
* `destination_account` (string, required) The recipient of a possible payment.
* `ledger_hash` (string, optional) The unique hash of the ledger version to use.
* `ledger_index` (number or string, optional) The ledger index of the ledger to use, or a shortcut string such as `validated`, `closed` or `current`.
* `credentials` (array, optional) A set of credential ledger entry IDs to take into account when checking whether the sender can send funds to the destination.

## Returns

* `deposit_authorized` (boolean) Whether the source account is authorized to send payments directly to the destination account. `true` if the destination does not require deposit authorization or has preauthorized the source.
* `source_account` (string) The source account specified in the request.
* `destination_account` (string) The destination account specified in the request.
* `credentials` (array) The credentials specified in the request, if any.
* `ledger_hash` (string) The identifying hash of the ledger used to generate this response.
* `ledger_index` (number) The ledger index of the ledger version used.
* `ledger_current_index` (number) The ledger index of the current in-progress ledger, if used.
* `validated` (boolean) If `true`, the information comes from a validated ledger version.
